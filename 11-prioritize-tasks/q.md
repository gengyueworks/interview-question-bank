# Prioritize tasks with multiple deadlines / 任务优先级

## Question
> **How do you prioritize tasks when you have multiple deadlines?**

## Sample Answer
> "I prioritize tasks by assessing their urgency and importance. I create a task list, assign deadlines, and break down larger projects into smaller, manageable steps. Effective time management, delegation when possible, and regular progress checks help me ensure all deadlines are met."

## Key Expressions
- **assessing their urgency and importance** — 评估紧急性和重要性
- **create a task list** — 创建任务列表
- **assign deadlines** — 设定截止日期
- **break down larger projects into smaller, manageable steps** — 将大项目分解为更小的、可管理的步骤
- **Effective time management** — 有效的时间管理
- **delegation when possible** — 可能时进行委派
- **regular progress checks** — 定期进度检查
- **ensure all deadlines are met** — 确保所有截止日期都得到满足

## Tips
- **提到 Eisenhower 矩阵**（紧急/重要四象限）会显得专业
- 展示你有**系统化的方法**而不是凭感觉
- 可以提到使用的工具：Notion / Trello / JIRA / Excel
- **委派（delegation）** 是一个很好的关键词，说明你不是一个人扛
