# Tell me about a time you failed / 失败经历

## Question
> **Tell me about a time you failed and what you learned from it.**

## Sample Answer
> "One notable failure occurred when I was managing a project that ended up exceeding the budget due to unforeseen challenges. It was a difficult situation, but I took full responsibility, communicated transparently with stakeholders, and devised a cost-cutting plan. This experience taught me the importance of thorough risk assessment and proactive problem-solving, skills I've since honed to prevent similar issues in the future."

## Key Expressions
- **One notable failure occurred when ...** — 一次值得注意的失败发生在……
- **exceeding the budget due to unforeseen challenges** — 因不可预见的挑战超出了预算
- **took full responsibility** — 承担全部责任
- **communicated transparently with stakeholders** — 与利益相关者透明沟通
- **devised a cost-cutting plan** —— 制定了一个削减成本的计划
- **taught me the importance of ...** —— 让我明白了……的重要性
- **thorough risk assessment** —— 彻底的风险评估
- **proactive problem-solving** —— 主动解决问题
- **skills I've since honed** —— 我此后磨练的技能

## ⭐ STAR 法则拆解
| 要素 | 内容 |
|------|------|
| **S**ituation | 管理一个项目 |
| **T**ask | 控制预算 |
| **A**ction | 承担责任 → 透明沟通 → 制定削减计划 |
| **R**esult | 学到了风险评估和主动解决问题的技能 |

## Tips
- **失败本身不重要，重要的是你从中学到了什么**
- 必须展示**反思和改进**，不能只是"我失败了然后结束了"
- 选择**可挽回的失败**，不是那种彻底搞砸无法收拾的
- 结尾一定要回到**现在的你因此变得更好**
