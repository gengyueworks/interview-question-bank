# Persuade a team to adopt your idea / 说服团队

## Question
> **Describe a situation where you had to persuade a team to adopt your idea.**

## Sample Answer
> "In a previous role, I proposed a new project management software to improve efficiency. To persuade the team, I conducted research to highlight the benefits, presented a clear implementation plan, and invited team members to share their input. By addressing concerns and demonstrating the positive impact, we gained unanimous support for the change."

## Key Expressions
- **proposed a new ... to improve efficiency** — 提议新的……以提高效率
- **conducted research to highlight the benefits** — 进行研究以突出好处
- **presented a clear implementation plan** — 展示清晰的实施计划
- **invited team members to share their input** — 邀请团队成员分享他们的意见
- **addressing concerns** — 解决顾虑
- **demonstrating the positive impact** — 展示积极影响
- **gained unanimous support for the change** — 获得了对该变革的一致支持

## ⭐ STAR 法则拆解
| 要素 | 内容 |
|------|------|
| **S**ituation | 团队需要提高项目管理效率 |
| **T**ask | 说服团队采用新软件 |
| **A**ction | 调研数据 → 展示方案 → 邀请反馈 → 解决顾虑 |
| **R**esult | 全票通过 |

## Tips
- **说服力 = 数据 + 共情 + 参与**
- 关键不是"我说服了他们"，而是"我们一起找到了更好的方案"
- 展示你**倾听**了反对意见并认真回应
- 数据/案例比主观判断更有说服力
