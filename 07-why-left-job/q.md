# Why did you leave your last job? / 离职原因

## Question
> **Why did you leave your last job?**

## Sample Answer
> "I left my last job because I felt that I had outgrown the opportunities it offered. I was seeking new challenges and a role that aligned better with my career goals. I wanted to contribute my skills and expertise to a company where I could make a more significant impact, which is why I'm excited about the opportunity with your organization."

## Key Expressions
- **outgrown the opportunities it offered** — 已经超越了它能提供的机会
- **seeking new challenges** — 寻求新的挑战
- **aligned better with my career goals** — 更符合我的职业目标
- **contribute my skills and expertise** — 贡献我的技能和专业能力
- **make a more significant impact** — 产生更大的影响
- **excited about the opportunity with your organization** — 对贵组织的机会感到兴奋

## Tips
- **正面表述**: 即使离职原因不愉快，也要正面表达
- **聚焦未来**: 重点放在"为什么选择你们"而不是"为什么离开他们"
- **绝对禁止**: 说前公司/前老板坏话
- **安全理由**: 寻求成长 / 职业转型 / 行业兴趣 / 地理因素 / 公司裁员
