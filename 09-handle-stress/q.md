# How do you handle stress or pressure? / 压力管理

## Question
> **How do you handle stress or pressure?**

## Sample Answer
> "I handle stress and pressure by first staying organized and breaking down tasks into manageable steps. I also prioritize effectively and maintain open communication with my team to ensure everyone is aligned and informed. Additionally, I make it a point to take short breaks to recharge and maintain a healthy work-life balance. My ability to adapt and remain calm under uncertainty has allowed me to successfully navigate challenging projects."

## Key Expressions
- **staying organized and breaking down tasks into manageable steps** — 保持条理并将任务分解为可管理的步骤
- **prioritize effectively** — 有效确定优先级
- **maintain open communication with my team** — 与团队保持开放沟通
- **ensure everyone is aligned and informed** — 确保每个人都达成共识并知情
- **take short breaks to recharge** — 短暂休息充电
- **maintain a healthy work-life balance** — 保持健康的工作生活平衡
- **adapt and remain calm under uncertainty** — 在不确定性中适应并保持冷静
- **successfully navigate challenging projects** — 成功驾驭具有挑战性的项目

## Tips
- **展示方法论**: 不是"我能扛压力"，而是"我有方法管理压力"
- **具体策略**: 分解任务 / 设优先级 / 沟通同步 / 适当休息 / 运动
- **举一个简短例子**会让回答更有说服力
- 避免说"我从来不感到压力"——这不真实
