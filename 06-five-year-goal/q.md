# Where do you see yourself in 5 years? / 五年规划

## Question
> **Where do you see yourself in 5 years?**

## Sample Answer
> "In five years, I see myself in a leadership role within the company, possibly in a senior project manager position. I hope to have developed deep expertise in our industry and to be mentoring and leading teams to success while contributing to the company's long-term goals."

## Key Expressions
- **see myself in a leadership role within the company** — 看自己在公司内担任领导角色
- **senior project manager position** — 高级项目经理职位
- **developed deep expertise in ...** — 在……领域深耕出深厚专业能力
- **mentoring and leading teams to success** — 指导并带领团队取得成功
- **contributing to the company's long-term goals** — 为公司长期目标做贡献

## Tips
- **展示成长意愿**: 表明你有上进心但不是"用你当跳板"
- **与公司对齐**: 回答要暗示你会长期留在这个公司发展
- **合理范围**: 不要说"我想取代你的位置"，也不要说"没想过"
- **安全公式**: "在这个领域深耕 → 带团队/带项目 → 为公司创造更大价值"
