# Difficult decision at work / 困难决策

## Question
> **Can you discuss a situation where you had to make a difficult decision at work?**

## Sample Answer
> "Certainly. I was once tasked with deciding whether to allocate additional resources to salvage a project that was falling behind schedule. After conducting a thorough analysis, I made the tough decision to reallocate resources, which temporarily impacted other projects. In the end, the project was successfully completed, and I learned the importance of making data-driven decisions."

## Key Expressions
- **tasked with deciding whether to ...** — 负责决定是否……
- **allocate additional resources** — 分配额外资源
- **salvage a project** — 挽救一个项目
- **falling behind schedule** — 进度落后
- **conducting a thorough analysis** — 进行彻底分析
- **made the tough decision to ...** — 做出了艰难的决定……
- **reallocate resources** — 重新分配资源
- **temporarily impacted other projects** — 暂时影响了其他项目
- **data-driven decisions** — 数据驱动的决策

## ⭐ STAR 法则拆解
| 要素 | 内容 |
|------|------|
| **S**ituation | 项目进度落后，需要决定是否追加资源 |
| **T**ask | 做出一个会影响多个项目的艰难决定 |
| **A**ction | 彻底分析 → 决定重新分配资源 |
| **R**esult | 项目成功完成 + 学会了数据驱动决策 |

## Tips
- **困难决策的关键在于过程透明**: 说明你是怎么分析的，而不只是结果
- 展示你能**权衡利弊**（trade-off）
- 如果决策有副作用（影响了其他项目），诚实承认并说明如何缓解
- "Data-driven" 是面试中的黄金词汇
