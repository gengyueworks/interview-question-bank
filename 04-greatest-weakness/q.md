# What's your greatest weakness? / 最大弱点

## Question
> **What's your greatest weakness?**

## Sample Answer
> "I used to struggle with delegating tasks, often taking on too much myself. However, I recognized this as a weakness and actively worked on improving my delegation skills. I now understand the importance of empowering team members and have seen how it enhances both productivity and team morale."

## Key Expressions
- **used to struggle with ...** — 曾经在……方面有困难
- **taking on too much myself** — 承担太多事情
- **recognized this as a weakness** — 认识到这是一个弱点
- **actively worked on improving ...** — 积极努力改进……
- **understand the importance of ...** — 理解了……的重要性
- **empowering team members** — 赋能团队成员
- **enhances both productivity and team morale** — 提升生产力和团队士气

## Tips
- **真实但可控**: 说一个真实的弱点，但必须是可改进的
- **必须包含改进措施**: "我曾经……但我已经……" 是标准结构
- **避免致命弱点**: 不要说"我不擅长沟通""我经常迟到"
- **好的弱点示例**: delegation / public speaking / saying no / impatience with slow progress
- **绝对不要说**: "我最大的弱点是太完美主义了"（太 cliché）
